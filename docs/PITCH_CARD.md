# Pitch card (3–5 min) — Team Titan / AgroLink

Print or keep open on phone. Details: `JUDGE_FEEDBACK.md` · demo steps: `DEMO_REHEARSAL.md`.

## Open (20 sec)

> We help peri-urban Dodowa-corridor farmers move **tomato and leafy greens** to **Accra restaurants and chop bars** by solving **same-day pickup, MoMo settlement, and proof of delivery**.

Not another marketplace. Not a TikTok clone. The feed is how kitchens discover stock — the product is **fulfillment + money + accountability**.

## Problem (30 sec)

- Tomato and leafies spoil fast on the Dodowa → Accra belt  
- Farmers sell farm-gate / market with uncertain pickup  
- Kitchens waste mornings chasing quality and price  
- No shared proof when produce arrives wrong or late  

*(Only use a spoilage story from your outreach table — never invent %.)*

## Solution walk (2 min) — demo this

1. **Farmer** lists photo + qty + pickup window (Studio)  
2. **Kitchen** finds it in the feed → cart → **pays MoMo first**  
3. **Driver** gets the paid job → accept → pickup → live track  
4. **POD photo** at the door → escrow releases → farmer payout  

Say once: *“Pay first, then match — so drivers chase real money, not ghosts.”*

## Trust (30 sec)

- Listing + comment moderation (AI + rules)  
- Driver Ghana Card / KYC — **admin approves**  
- Live track + POD photo  
- Disputes / refunds — **human admin**, not fully automatic  

Alerts: **in-app + push** (+ email if on file). No SMS. No WhatsApp in the product.

## Close / ask (20 sec)

> We’re validating with Dodowa growers and Accra kitchens on one corridor. Next: more paid same-day runs and sharper dispute SOP — not new crops or cold storage yet.

## If judges ask

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| Why not consumers? | B2B kitchens — volume, morning windows, MoMo |
| Why not AI pricing? | Optional tip only; farmer sets price |
| Why not WhatsApp? | In-app + push for MVP; phone for trip calls |

| Proof you talked to users? | Cite rows from `OUTREACH_CONTACTS.md` |
