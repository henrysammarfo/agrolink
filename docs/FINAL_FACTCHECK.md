| Claim | Verdict | Evidence / source | What to say |
|-------|---------|-------------------|-------------|
| Tomato has high PHL in Ghana (~20–50%; often ~30%) | **Supported** | HortiFresh economic analysis: tomato PHL often **20–50%** ([PDF](https://www.hortifresh.org/wp-content/uploads/Tomato-post-harvest-losses-in-Ghana_an-economic-analysis-002.pdf)); MoFA / press have cited **~30%** of tomato production | “Tomato is among Ghana’s highest post-harvest-loss crops; literature often cites roughly 20–50%, with ~30% used in public MoFA messaging.” |
| “80% tomato loss” | **Do not use** | Tavily answer floated high ranges; not a clean primary cite for pitch | Stick to **20–50% / ~30%**. |
| Worst tomato *production* belts = Ashanti / middle belt (Akumadan, Techiman) shipping to Accra | **Supported** | HortiFresh surveyed Akumadan / Afrancho / Tuobodom; retail sampling in Agbogbloshie, Makola, Madina | “National tomato loss is worst on long-haul belts into Accra. Our MVP is the Accra-end / peri-urban hop, not replacing Akumadan overnight.” |
| Greater Accra veggies also lose (tomato highest among common veg) | **Supported** | UG thesis on GAR vegetables: farmer estimates ~**20% tomato**, ~12–13% pepper/okra/cabbage, garden eggs much lower ([UGSpace](https://ugspace.ug.edu.gh/items/991162ff-cafc-4c7a-942d-1ed1de7af59c)) | Good for “our corridor still loses tomato/leafies if they don’t move same day.” |
| Leafy / indigenous veg lose fast (30–50% under poor ambient handling in SSA lit) | **Directionally supported** | Frontiers horticulture review on Ghana indigenous veg / PHL ([Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/horticulture/articles/10.3389/fhort.2025.1619846/full)); FAO-style 30–50% for some perishables | Use for kontomire/kale narrative; don’t invent a Dodowa-only % without a survey. |
| Accra produce logistics run pre-dawn; speed critical | **Supported** | Accra Street Journal: trucks **midnight–5am**; perishables must move fast; phone coordination; MoMo creeping in ([ASJ](https://accrastreetjournal.com/2026/02/25/inside-the-hidden-logistics-system-powering-accra-ghanas-busy-markets/)) | Supports restaurant morning procurement pain + same-day dispatch. |
| Madina / Agbogbloshie / Makola are major Accra produce hubs | **Supported** | HortiFresh + ASJ + market guides | Closest to Dodowa farms: **Dodowa → Ashaiman/Tema → Madina**; Agbogbloshie = big wholesale, not “closest.” |
| Food-service (restaurants/chop bars) is a real buyer for farm-direct / aggregator models | **Supported** | Local players pitch restaurants/chop bars: [Merdeo](https://merdeofoods.com/), [Sesi Foods](https://sesifoods.com/), [Urban Jungle](https://urbanjungleagro.com/); USDA FAS Ghana HRI reports | ICP is credible; competitors prove demand exists. |

### Seller-side validation checklist (do this week)

Talk to **≥5 farmers** (Dodowa / Ashaiman / Tema peri-urban) and **≥5 buyers** (chop bar / restaurant / caterer):

1. What do you harvest weekly (crops + volumes)?  
2. Where do you sell today (Dodowa / Madina / Agbogbloshie / farm gate)?  
3. When does produce spoil if unsold?  
4. Would you accept a **driver pickup** if MoMo lands same day?  
5. Preferred alert: **SMS / WhatsApp / call**? (Expect SMS/WhatsApp — not email.)  
6. For buyers: how do you procure mornings; what fails (time, price, quality, no-show)?

## TikTok feed — refined justification (judge-safe)

**Buyers:** Accra food-service staff and young operators are phone-first; vertical photo feed reduces decision time vs dense directories (discovery, not innovation).

**Sellers:** Not a TikTok creator tool. Seller path = **photo + quantity + price + pickup window**; AI only assists metadata. Validate with farmers that this is faster than WhatsApp broadcasting.

**Pitch line:**  
*“The feed is how chefs discover today’s harvest. The hard system is match → driver → MoMo → POD.”*
