## Patterns AgroLink already matches

- Full-bleed produce media + right-rail social actions + sticky **Add to cart**
- Live map tracking + driver card (Bolt/Uber)
- MoMo Paystack (local Uber Eats equivalent)

## Patterns to keep pushing (TikTok citizens)

1. **One primary verb** per screen — Add to cart / Pay with MoMo / Track driver
2. **ETA as clock time** (“by 5:40pm”) not only minutes
3. **Progress strip** on track: Matched → Picking up → On the way → Delivered
4. **Huge tap targets** on budget Androids (iTel/Tecno ~360×640)
5. **No jargon** — “Service fee” not “platform fee”; “Driver” not “courier logistics”
